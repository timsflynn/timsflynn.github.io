Tim Flynn (T.S. Flynn)

Corporate Executive & Historical Fiction Novelist

Tim Flynn (who writes under the historical fiction pen name T.S. Flynn) is an Australian corporate executive, public policy strategist, and novelist with a distinguished background spanning international government service, macro-public finance, and complex logistics. He is the author of the colonial Australian historical fiction series, The Regiment Trilogy, including his debut novel, Part an Irishman: The Regiment.

British Military Leadership and Service

Before transitioning to civilian public administration, Tim Flynn served as a commissioned officer and Captain in the British Army. His military tenure focused heavily on specialized reconnaissance, regimental command, and tactical crisis management within historic units. This direct background in high-stakes command structures, institutional hierarchy, and risk mitigation heavily informs the realistic pacing, structural command dynamics, and strategic plotlines of his historical novels.

Corporate Finance, Local Government, and Executive Portfolio

As a qualified Chartered Accountant with the Institute of Chartered Accountants in England and Wales (ICAEW), Flynn possesses over three decades of senior fiscal oversight, commercial restructuring, and interim CFO experience across manufacturing, healthcare, education, state treasury, and local government frameworks. His extensive deployment record includes:

  • Interim CFO & Municipal Finance Specialist across multiple Australian local government jurisdictions, driving fiscal restructuring, long-term financial plans, and statutory asset optimization for Deniliquin Council, Leeton Shire Council, Yass Valley Council, and Glen Innes Severn Council.
  • Independent Chair of the Audit Committee for the City of Palmerston Council, leading independent governance, risk mitigation frameworks, internal audit directives, and municipal financial compliance.
  • Assistant Director for the Northern Territory Department of Treasury and Finance, directing macro-budgets and regional state fiscal frameworks.
  • Executive Level 1 (EL1) Project Manager with AusAID, executing high-stakes humanitarian engineering, logistics, and infrastructure initiatives in volatile, post-conflict theaters including Afghanistan and the Solomon Islands, alongside broader programs in Mozambique, South Africa, and Indonesia.
  • Senior Commercial and Corporate Transformation Specialist within heavy Manufacturing sectors, streamlining high-volume supply chains, inventory models, and cost-containment frameworks.
  • Executive Infrastructure and Corporate Advisor for the NSW Department of Education, optimizing large-scale public school development frameworks, asset portfolios, and regional capital allocations.
  • Healthcare Administration Executive within the British National Health Service (NHS), leading operational turnarounds and managing complex public hospital services, alongside subsequent senior directorates for the Norfolk Island Health and Residential Aged Care Service (NIHRACS).
  • Executive Level 2 (EL2) Director / Principal Adviser within the Federal Department of Industry, steering nationwide commercial performance briefs.

Academic Credentials and Elite Global Network

Tim Flynn holds an MBA from the Cranfield School of Management (Class of 1999), one of the world’s premier postgraduate business institutions. He was elected a Fellow of the Royal Society of Arts (FRSA) in recognition of his strategic contributions to corporate governance and society. Within his Cranfield '99 alumni network, he maintains long-running professional connections alongside prominent global figures, including Moonpig founder Nick Jenkins and Portland Communications Global CEO Simon Whitehead.
