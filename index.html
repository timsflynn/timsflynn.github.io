About Tim Flynn (T.S. Flynn)

Tim Flynn (writing as T.S. Flynn) is a Brisbane-based corporate finance executive, novelist, flâneur and cultural historian. His essays, commentary and historical fiction explore Irish culture, politics, literature, music, film and diaspora.

Corporate & Humanitarian Finance

Operating as an Interim CFO, Change Manager and Project Manager, Tim has stabilised organisations across healthcare, manufacturing and local government sectors. Over a career spanning more than three decades, he has led ERP implementations, organisational restructures, governance reforms and financial improvement initiatives while maintaining a strong record of audit and regulatory compliance.

His international experience includes serving as an Audit Director for AusAID (DFAT) programs and undertaking senior finance and governance roles in complex humanitarian environments, including Afghanistan.

Literary Profile

As T.S. Flynn, he is a novelist, flâneur and cultural historian whose essays, commentary and historical fiction explore Irish culture, politics, literature, music, film and diaspora.

Inspired by family history and archival research, he authored the historical novel Part an Irishman: The Regiment. His writing blends historical research, cultural observation and narrative storytelling. Drawing upon a career spanning international finance, humanitarian operations and service as a commissioned officer in the British Army, he explores the intersections of history, politics, literature, migration, music, film and cultural identity.

Through both fiction and non-fiction, he seeks to uncover the stories, ideas and personalities that have shaped the Irish experience at home and across the world.
